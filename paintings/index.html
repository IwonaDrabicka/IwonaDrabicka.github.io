Art
Some paintings feel like places.
Others feel like a memory of one.
Between structure and nature, between the immediate and the slow, these paintings explore different ways of seeing and feeling.
One technique asks me to act in the moment. The material dries quickly, leaving little room for hesitation or correction. What happens on the canvas becomes a record of that particular moment — instinctive, immediate and impossible to fully repeat.
The other unfolds much more slowly. A painting can take days or weeks, growing layer by layer. It becomes a conversation between colours, ideas and the image I carry in my mind — a process of looking, changing, waiting and discovering what I want to bring onto the canvas.
Take your time.
Look for what draws you in.
Perhaps you will find something familiar — a place, a season, a feeling you once knew.
Stay with the paintings that speak to you.
